  - 25% normalized Sharpe (tanh(sharpe/3))

  - 15% Profit Factor (tanh((pf-1)/1.5))
  - 15% Plateau ratio (neighborhood stability)
  - 10% Trade count quality
  - 10% Cost stress (Sharpe after 2x fees)
  - 5% Win rate quality
  - minus drawdown penalty

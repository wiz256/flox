### Critical FLOX API discoveries (from reading source and debugging):

1. Symbol registration:  registerSymbol(exchange, symbol)  does NOT populate  _symbols  map — Strategy constructor fails
with "Symbol X not found in registry". Must use  registerSymbol(SymbolInfo{...}) .
2. Order semantics:
  •  emitMarketBuy(sym, qty)  → Opens/reduces to long position
  •  emitMarketSell(sym, qty)  → Opens/reduces to short position (does NOT close a long!)
  •  emitClosePosition(sym)  → Reduce-only close: checks  position() , creates SELL for long or BUY for short with
  reduceOnly = true
  • This is why  claude-glm  works (uses  emitClosePosition ) and our code didn't (used  emitMarketSell  for exits)
3. Strategy dispatch:  Strategy::onBar()  is  final  — dispatches to  onSymbolBar()  or  onSymbolTrade() . Never
override  onBar() .
4. Two backtest paths:
  •  runner.runBars(BarEvents)  → dispatches  onSymbolBar  with full OHLCV from  BarEvent.bar  — fast (0.6ms/run for 11K
  bars)
  •  runner.run(OhlcvReplaySource)  → dispatches  onSymbolTrade  with synthetic trades from close prices — slow
  (~60ms/run) and requires parallel CSV bar pointer hack for real OHLCV
